Privacy Policy

Adaptive Fitness Coach
Effective date: August 10, 2026

Adaptive Fitness Coach (“Adaptive Fitness,” “we,” “our,” or “the App”) is a fitness coaching application designed to provide personalized workout recommendations, guided exercise instructions, and training progress tracking. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App may collect, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you choose to use, Adaptive Fitness may collect or process:

  • Fitness profile information you provide, such as age, sex, height, weight, fitness goals, workout preferences, available workout time, and preferred body areas to emphasize.
  • Workout information, including exercises, sets, repetitions, resistance or weight used, perceived difficulty, and workout history.
  • Photos you choose to provide through the equipment-scanning feature for the purpose of identifying gym equipment and providing exercise guidance.
  • WHOOP data, if you choose to connect your WHOOP account, including Recovery data, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, Sleep data, Cycle/Strain data, Workout data, and body measurements such as height, weight, and maximum heart rate.

How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve the App’s fitness-coaching functionality. This may include:

  • Generating and adapting workout recommendations.
  • Adjusting suggested training volume or intensity based on recovery, sleep, strain, and recent activity.
  • Remembering prior workouts and resistance levels to support progression.
  • Providing guidance regarding gym equipment and exercise technique.
  • Improving the functionality, reliability, and user experience of the App.

WHOOP Data

If you connect your WHOOP account, access occurs only after you authorize Adaptive Fitness through WHOOP’s authorization process. Adaptive Fitness will request only the WHOOP permissions needed for the features offered by the App.

WHOOP information is used to personalize fitness and workout recommendations. Adaptive Fitness does not sell, rent, or license WHOOP data to third parties and does not use WHOOP data for advertising.

Sharing of Information

Adaptive Fitness does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers solely as needed to operate app functionality, such as secure hosting, authentication, data storage, or AI-powered equipment analysis. Such providers are expected to process information only for the services they provide to Adaptive Fitness and subject to applicable privacy and security requirements.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the App, or others from fraud, abuse, security threats, or unlawful activity.

Data Security

We intend to use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate for the information processed by the App, including encrypted HTTPS connections for data transmitted between the App, our services, and connected services such as WHOOP. Credentials and access tokens should not be stored in publicly accessible source code.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App’s functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. The specific retention period may depend on the type of information and the feature being used.

Your Choices and Controls

You may choose not to connect WHOOP or not to use features that require particular information. If WHOOP is connected, you may disconnect or revoke Adaptive Fitness’s access through available WHOOP account or integration controls. When access is revoked, Adaptive Fitness will no longer retrieve new information from your WHOOP account.

You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information held by Adaptive Fitness, subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

Health and Fitness Information

Adaptive Fitness provides general fitness and exercise guidance and is not a medical service. Information and recommendations provided by the App are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Users should exercise within their abilities and seek appropriate professional medical guidance when necessary.

Children

Adaptive Fitness is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App develops or as legal, technical, or product requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or requests concerning personal information, please contact the email address provided as the support contact for Adaptive Fitness Coach in the WHOOP Developer Dashboard.
